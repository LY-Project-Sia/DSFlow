08 / FAQ

QUESTIONS?
ANSWERED.

Your booking experience shows the selected court, date, time, duration, players and full price breakdown before confirmation.

What are the current rates?

Badminton is configured at ₱350 per court, Pickleball at ₱500 per court, with an ₱85 entrance fee per person. These are the values present in your supplied site.

What are the operating hours?

DragonSmashPH is configured for daily play from 6:00 AM–2:00 AM.

How does booking work?

Select sport, date, court, duration and players, choose an available time, then enter your contact details and confirm.

Can I book multiple hours?

Yes. The interface supports 1, 2 or 3-hour sessions and checks for overlapping reservations.

Can I host tournaments or group sessions?

Yes. The events section is designed for tournaments, private group bookings and community sessions. Connect the inquiry form to your preferred email or CRM when deploying.

Does DragonSmashPH have an admin portal?

Yes. Open Staff / Admin Portal from the footer to manage bookings, court status, rates, operating hours, customers, reports, messages and website settings. This demo uses browser storage; production deployment should connect the portal to a shared backend.
